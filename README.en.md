## Translation as inheritance

> **“路漫漫其修远兮，吾将上下而求索。”**  
> — Qu Yuan, *[Li Sao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Sao)* — “Long is the road ahead; I will search high and low.”

Machines take the first step; people decide how far the words can travel. Each translation records its model, terminology decisions, and human review. Anyone may correct a mistranslation, repair an equation, refine the prose, or adopt a paper that still needs a reader.

Every change should help the next person see farther without erasing the footsteps that came before.

## Rights before publication

Translation is an adaptation. A full translation is published only when the source license permits adaptation or the rightsholder gives clear written permission. Papers with uncertain rights remain visible through their original links and project status, but do not cross the publication gate.

This is an independent, nonprofit community project. It does not represent Snowmass, SLAC, arXiv, any author, collaboration, or publisher. The complete boundary is recorded in the [rights and publication protocol](docs/RIGHTS_PROTOCOL.md).

## Walk with us

You do not need to translate an entire paper. One term, one equation, or one clearer paragraph can become part of this map.

- Visit the [public atlas](https://snowmass-physics-cn.netlify.app/?lang=en) and find a paper that matters to you.
- Read the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) and propose a translation, review, or evidence update.
- Leave sources and reasoning in every Pull Request so the work remains traceable.
- Contact: [WangTheoPhys@outlook.com](mailto:WangTheoPhys@outlook.com).
